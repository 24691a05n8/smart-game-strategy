📋 Decision Steps

How AO* Works:

AND nodes require ALL sub-goals to be achieved. Cost = sum of children costs.

OR nodes need ONE sub-goal to be achieved. Cost = min of children costs.

AO* propagates costs bottom-up and selects the optimal solution graph.
